description: "Your handshake reply lists the features your site supports. RankHouse only calls what you list."

---

The `capabilities` list in your [handshake](/endpoints/handshake) reply tells RankHouse which endpoints you built. RankHouse sends requests only to those endpoints, and the Custom Website card shows the list under **Supported features**.

```json
{
  "name": "My website",
  "version": "1.0.0",
  "capabilities": ["posts.create", "posts.update", "seo", "pages.list"],
  "challenge_response": "..."
}
```

## The list

| Capability | Endpoints you build | What it switches on | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `posts.create` | [`POST /posts`](/endpoints/create-post) | **Required.** New blog posts on your site | Blog publishing |
| `posts.update` | [`PUT /posts/{id}`](/endpoints/update-post) | **Required.** Changes to posts RankHouse already sent | Blog updates |
| `seo` | [`PUT /seo`](/endpoints/seo) | New titles and descriptions on pages you already have | SEO updates |
| `pages.list` | [`GET /pages`](/endpoints/pages) | Your page ids matched to the pages RankHouse found on your site, and the **Sync pages** button | Page listing |
| `blog_categories` | [`GET /blog-categories`](/endpoints/categories-and-authors) | Your categories in the RankHouse editor | Blog categories |
| `authors` | [`GET /authors`](/endpoints/categories-and-authors) | Your authors in the RankHouse editor | Authors |

Names are case-sensitive.

<Note>
RankHouse won't connect unless the list includes both `posts.create` and `posts.update`. Names it doesn't know are dropped without an error, so after connecting, check that **Supported features** shows everything you meant to list.
</Note>

## Features you leave out

- RankHouse never sends a request to an endpoint you didn't list.
- The feature stays off for the site owner. For example, without `seo`, new page titles stay in RankHouse instead of reaching your site.

## Adding a feature later

1. Build the endpoint.
2. Add its name to the `capabilities` list in your handshake reply.
3. Ask the site owner to press **Re-verify** on the Custom Website card.

RankHouse runs the handshake again and saves the new list. The key doesn't change.
