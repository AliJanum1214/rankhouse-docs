RankHouse writes blog posts and better page titles and descriptions for a website. On WordPress, Shopify and Webflow it connects without any code. For every other site (Laravel, Django, Rails, Next.js, a headless CMS or a CMS you built yourself) there is the **publishing API**.

You add a few HTTPS endpoints to your site. When the site owner publishes in RankHouse, our servers send a signed request to those endpoints, and your code saves the content wherever your site reads it from.

```text
Site owner presses Publish in RankHouse
          │
          ▼
RankHouse servers ── signed HTTPS request ──▶ https://your-site.com/rankhouse/posts
                                              (your code, your database)
```

You never call RankHouse and you never poll. RankHouse calls you.

## What you build

Three endpoints are required. The rest each switch on one feature, so you can start small and add more later.

| Endpoint | Needed to connect | What it gives the site owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`POST /handshake`](/endpoints/handshake) | Yes | The connection itself |
| [`POST /posts`](/endpoints/create-post) | Yes | New blog posts on your site |
| [`PUT /posts/{id}`](/endpoints/update-post) | Yes | Changes to posts RankHouse already sent |
| [`PUT /seo`](/endpoints/seo) | No | New titles and descriptions on pages you already have |
| [`GET /pages`](/endpoints/pages) | No | Your page ids matched to the pages RankHouse found on your site |
| [`GET /blog-categories`, `GET /authors`](/endpoints/categories-and-authors) | No | Your own categories and authors in the RankHouse editor |

All of them sit under one base URL that you choose, such as `https://your-site.com/rankhouse`.

## What RankHouse can and can't do

- It calls only the endpoints your site lists in its handshake, and your code decides what to save.
- It has no login to your server, database or hosting. It knows one address and one key.
- It adds no scripts to your pages.
- It signs every request with your key, so your code can refuse anything that didn't come from RankHouse. See [Authentication](/authentication).
