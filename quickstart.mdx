---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Run a sample receiver, connect it to RankHouse and publish a test post."
icon: rocket
---

This takes about 20 minutes. You'll run one of our sample receivers on your machine, connect it to RankHouse, and watch a post arrive. Then you move the same endpoints into your real site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the key">
    In RankHouse, open **Account → Integrations**, press **Connect** on **Custom Website**, then press **Generate key**. Use **Show** or **Copy** to read it. The key is 64 characters long and belongs to one website.

    If you're the developer and someone else owns the RankHouse account, ask them for the key through a password manager or another private channel, not in a shared chat.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a sample receiver">
    Pick your language and start the sample with the key. Each sample is one file that answers every endpoint RankHouse uses.

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Node.js" icon="node-js" href="/samples/node" />
      <Card title="Python" icon="python" href="/samples/python" />
      <Card title="PHP" icon="php" href="/samples/php" />
    </CardGroup>

    For example, with Node.js:

    ```bash
    npm install express
    RANKHOUSE_API_KEY=your-64-character-key node server.js
    # RankHouse receiver on http://localhost:4000/rankhouse
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make it reachable">
    RankHouse's servers send the requests, so they must be able to reach your base URL over the internet. They can't reach `localhost` on your laptop.

    Put the sample on an address **on your own domain**, over HTTPS. A staging server works, and so does a tunnel ([Cloudflare Tunnel](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/) or [ngrok](https://ngrok.com)) with a hostname on your domain. Your base URL is that address followed by `/rankhouse`, for example `https://staging.your-site.com/rankhouse`.

    <Warning>
    RankHouse remembers the domain a key first connects to (for example `https://your-site.com`) and refuses a different domain later, even after **Disconnect**. Any address on the same domain is fine: test on `https://staging.your-site.com` now, move to `https://www.your-site.com` later. A tunnel's own address, such as `abc123.ngrok-free.app`, counts as a different domain, so don't connect one to the account that will run your real site.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect">
    Paste the base URL into **API base URL** on the Custom Website card and press **Connect website**.

    RankHouse sends a random challenge to `POST /handshake`. Your server signs it with the key and replies with the features it supports. When the signature matches, the card shows **Connected** and lists the features under **Supported features**. If it fails, the card shows why. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish a test post">
    Open a blog post in RankHouse and press **Publish**. Your `POST /posts` endpoint receives it, saves it and answers with its id. In the sample, the new post shows up in `GET /pages`.

    New connections start on **Manual Publish**: RankHouse sends nothing to your site until someone presses Publish. The site owner can change this later on the same card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Move it into your site">
    Build the same endpoints on your real site, against your real database. Then:

    1. Put the key in your server's environment or secret store.
    2. Serve the endpoints over HTTPS.
    3. On the Custom Website card, press **Change**, paste the new base URL (on the same domain) and press **Connect website**.

    RankHouse runs the handshake against the new address before it sends anything there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Added an endpoint later? List it in your handshake reply and press **Re-verify** on the Custom Website card. The key stays the same. See [Capabilities](/capabilities).
</Tip>
