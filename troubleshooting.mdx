## Connecting

When **Connect website** or **Re-verify** fails, the Custom Website card shows one of these messages.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Could not reach https://…/handshake">
    RankHouse's servers couldn't open your address. Check that:

    - the base URL is right, including the path (for example `/rankhouse`), with no `/handshake` on the end,
    - the server is running and reachable from the internet, not only from your network,
    - the HTTPS certificate is valid,
    - no firewall, bot protection or login page sits in front of the endpoints.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Handshake failed with status 401 (or your own error message)">
    Your server answered with an error, usually from its own key or signature check.

    - **Wrong key.** The key on your server must match the one on the Custom Website card. After **Regenerate**, the old key stops working.
    - **Signature over the wrong bytes.** Sign the raw body exactly as it arrived, not JSON you parsed and serialised again. See [Authentication](/authentication).
    - **Wrong header names.** Read `X-RankHouse-Timestamp` and `X-RankHouse-Signature`.
    - **Clock.** If you refuse old timestamps, your server's clock must be right. Turn on NTP.

    When your handshake answers with `{ "error": "..." }`, that message is shown on the card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Handshake signature verification failed: the receiver did not sign the challenge with this brand's API key.">
    Your server answered, but `challenge_response` was wrong. It must be `HMAC-SHA256(key, challenge)` as **lowercase hex**, over the `challenge` string alone, made with the same key. See [Handshake](/endpoints/handshake).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The receiver must implement the required endpoints and declare: posts.create, posts.update">
    Your `capabilities` list is missing one or both required names. Add them, exactly as written. See [Capabilities](/capabilities).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No active custom-platform API key for this brand. Generate one first.">
    There's no key yet, or it was revoked with **Disconnect**. Press **Generate key**, put the new key on your server, then connect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="base_url must be an absolute http(s) URL">
    Paste the full address, starting with `https://`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This key is connected to https://…. To move to another domain, press Disconnect, generate a new key and connect again.">
    The connection is live on another domain, and RankHouse won't move a live connection by accident. Any address on the connected domain works with **Change**. To move to a different domain, press **Disconnect**, then **Generate key**, put the new key on your server and press **Connect website** with the new address. Posts sent to the old domain show as **Not published** again, so you can publish them to the new site.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Publishing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The post was saved on my site but RankHouse says it wasn't published">
    Your `POST /posts` reply must include the post's `id`, for example `{ "id": "1042" }`, with a `2xx` status. A reply without an id counts as a failed delivery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Some posts fail the signature check, most don't">
    Long posts can go over your server's request size limit, and the body is cut before your check runs. Raise the limit for these endpoints: Express allows 100 KB by default (`express.json({ limit: "5mb" })`), and nginx 1 MB (`client_max_body_size`).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The same post appears twice">
    RankHouse can send a post again. Save `source_id` with each post and, when it arrives again, update the post you have instead of making a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Posts arrive but don't show on the site">
    Check `status`. A post sent as `draft` is meant to stay off the live site until someone publishes it there. Which status RankHouse sends depends on the publishing mode the site owner picked on the Custom Website card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Categories or authors don't show in the editor">
    List `blog_categories` or `authors` in your handshake, then press **Re-verify**. Check the result under **Supported features**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New titles and descriptions don't reach my pages">
    You need the `seo` capability and a working [`PUT /seo`](/endpoints/seo). If your endpoint can't find pages, add [`GET /pages`](/endpoints/pages) so RankHouse can name each page by your id, then press **Sync pages**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Email [support@rankhouse.ai](mailto:support@rankhouse.ai) with your site's address and the message you see. We work with your developer until the site is connected.
